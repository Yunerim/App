Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Cookie app (the "App"), published by Geewen, handles your information. In short: the App does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

The App does not collect any personal information. It does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

The App does not use analytics tools, advertising, or tracking technologies, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

Data stored on your device

Any information the App needs in order to work is kept only on your own device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes it.

Third-party services

The App displays web content through your device's browser (Google Chrome or another compatible browser) and is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may automatically process technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the content and keep their services secure. That processing is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
